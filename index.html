Genre Popularity

This dashboard compares how five genres (Pop, Rock, Country / Folk, Hip hop and Jazz / Blues) are represented across release years in a 10,000-song sample of the Million Song Dataset, and how popular their artists are. The top chart counts the unique artists in each genre per year. The middle chart shows each genre's median artist.hotttnesss. The bar chart compares a selected year with the year before. Hover over a point on either line chart to see its exact values. Popularity scores reflect December 2010 rather than popularity at the time of release.
